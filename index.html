🌴 Jungle VR

  • In VR: aim the ray at things and press the trigger. Ground = teleport, animal = learn (with voice).
  • Left stick walk · right stick turn · grip brings the menu to you.
  • On desktop: drag to look · W A S D to move · click to interact · M brings the menu.
  • Try Day / Night (owls + glowing mushrooms), Canoe ride, Treetop view, and tap the red beacons to stop loggers and poachers.
